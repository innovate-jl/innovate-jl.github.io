Unifolllow was built to make Instagram export comparisons simpler without asking for a password or sending personal files to a server.

Ways to support the project

The most helpful support is using the tool responsibly and sharing it with people who need a privacy-first alternative to account-connected follower apps.

  • Share Unifolllow with friends who want to compare their own Instagram export.
  • Send us feedback about unclear steps or export formats that need support.
  • Share your experience so future visitors can learn from the community.

Two Regular College Students

Hello! Welcome to Unifolllow, created by two USC alumni who wanted a more private and transparent way to compare Instagram export files.

Many follower tools ask for an Instagram login or collect data on a server. We chose a different approach: you download your own information from Instagram and the comparison runs in your browser. That means Unifolllow does not need your password, account connection, or export upload.

Unifolllow is built specifically for Instagram export files. Select the follower and following files from one download, and the browser compares them locally. Our result methodology explains exactly what that comparison can—and cannot—tell you.

As creators, we bring different skills to the table but share the same goal of building an innovative and ethical solution. With our tech expertise and understanding of the digital world, we're committed to creating a safer and more authentic online experience for everyone.

ABOUT UNIFOLLOW

A small utility with a clear boundary.

Unifolllow helps people compare the follower and following lists in their own Instagram export without asking them to connect an account or hand over a password.

Product information · Last reviewed November 8, 2026

Why this exists

Many people want a simple answer to a narrow question: which accounts in an export are not mutual at that moment? The answer should not require an account-connected service, a browser extension, or a black-box claim about live Instagram activity. Unifolllow is built around the files a visitor already controls.

What the product does

The checker reads a selected following file and follower file set locally in the browser. It normalizes handles for comparison, removes duplicates, and shows the handles present in following but absent from followers. The export health report makes the file counts visible so a visitor can notice an incomplete selection before drawing a conclusion.

What the product does not do

It does not sign into Instagram, access an Instagram API, watch accounts over time, or establish why a person followed or unfollowed. A result is a file comparison from an export snapshot. The methodology and compatibility notes describe those limits in detail.

How the site is maintained

Documentation is tied to the checker’s supported formats and is reviewed when behavior changes. The library favors concrete guidance—what files to select, what an error means, and what the result can support—over generic advice about social media. Visitors can report a compatibility issue without sending personal exports.

Independence

Unifolllow is an independent utility and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Instagram’s export options may change; when they do, the supported-format documentation is the source of truth for this site.

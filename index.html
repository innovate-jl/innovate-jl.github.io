    HOW IT WORKS

    Three simple steps

    Use your own Instagram download—no account connection needed.

    1

    Request your data

    In Instagram, go to Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.

    2

    Choose followers

    Select Followers and following, set the date range to All time, then download the export as HTML or JSON.

    3

    Upload and compare

    Unzip the download and add every followers_# file and following file above. We’ll compare them on your device.

    Which exact files do I need?

    Upload every followers_#.html file with following.html, or every followers_#.json file with following.json. A saved unifolllow-non-mutual-YYYY-MM-DD.txt list is optional; it adds a change summary but never hides your current results.

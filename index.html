    HOW IT WORKS

    Three simple steps

    Use your own Instagram download—no account connection needed.

    1

    Request your data

    In Instagram, go to Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.

    2

    Choose followers

    Select Followers and following, set the date range to All time, then download the export as HTML or JSON.

    3

    Upload and compare

    Unzip the download and add followers_1 and following above. We’ll compare them on your device.

    Which exact files do I need?

    Upload either followers_1.html with following.html, or followers_1.json with following.json. You can also add a previous known-following-YYYY-MM-DD.txt file to show only newly identified accounts.

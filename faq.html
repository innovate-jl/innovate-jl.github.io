How does Unifolllow work?

Unifolllow compares the accounts you follow on Instagram with your current followers. If you return with a saved known-following file, it can show newly identified accounts that do not follow you back.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your follower export is processed locally on your device. We do not store your file, ask for an Instagram login, or save your results.

Do I need to log in to use Unifolllow?

No. You do not need to create an account or log in. Download your Instagram information, then compare the exported files in your browser.

Can I use Unifolllow on mobile?

Yes. The checker works on desktop and mobile browsers. Download your Instagram information, unzip the export, and select the two required files.

What file formats are supported?

Unifolllow supports Instagram follower exports in HTML or JSON format: followers_1.html and following.html, or followers_1.json and following.json.

How accurate is Unifolllow?

Results reflect the two Instagram export files you provide. For the clearest current result, request a new export from Instagram and choose the All time date range.

What do I do if something is not working?

First check that the files have the expected names and are from the same Instagram export. If you are still stuck, use the contact and support page and we’ll help troubleshoot.

What does Unifolllow compare?

It compares the accounts in the following file you select with the accounts in the follower files you select. A result means a handle appears in the first list but not the second list from that export snapshot.

Does this tell me who unfollowed me?

No. A single export cannot prove when or why a change happened. It shows a difference between two lists at the time Instagram prepared the export. A saved baseline can summarize changes between two local snapshots, but it is not an event history.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The comparison happens in your browser. Unifolllow does not ask for an Instagram login, account connection, or password, and the checker does not transmit the files you select to a Unifolllow server.

Which files are supported?

Select either following.html with every followers_#.html file, or following.json with every followers_#.json file. Keep the files from one downloaded archive and use one format at a time.

Why do I need every numbered follower file?

Instagram can split a large follower list into several numbered files. Leaving one out makes the follower list incomplete and can produce a misleading comparison. The export health report shows how many files and accounts the checker read.

What does the saved list do?

Saving downloads the current non-mutual list as a text file. Adding it during a later comparison keeps the full current result visible and reports how many entries are new or no longer present relative to that saved snapshot.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes, if your browser can unzip the download and select the original files. The same privacy and file-format rules apply on mobile and desktop.

What should I send to support?

Send the selected filenames, whether you used HTML or JSON, your browser, and the exact error message. Do not send an export archive, credentials, or account handles.

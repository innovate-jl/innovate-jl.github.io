PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Unifolllow is designed so that the export comparison happens in your browser. The site does not require an Instagram login or account connection.

Files selected in the checker

When you select supported follower and following files, the checker reads them locally in your browser to calculate the comparison. The site does not upload those selected files or the resulting list to a Unifolllow server. A saved list is downloaded to your device only when you choose to save it.

Information we do not request

The checker does not ask for your Instagram password, session cookie, account token, or permission to access your Instagram account. Do not send those details to Unifolllow. We also ask that you do not send export archives or lists of account handles in a support message.

Site analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate use of public pages, such as visits and page interactions. Google may process technical information such as browser, device, IP-derived location, and cookie identifiers under its own policies. You can use browser privacy controls, content blockers, or Google’s available opt-out controls to limit analytics. Analytics is separate from the local export comparison.

Contact messages

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in the message is sent to the service used to receive that message. Send only what is necessary to describe a technical issue: browser, export format, filenames, and an error message are usually enough. Do not include passwords, export files, or sensitive relationship information.

External links

Links to third-party services, including Instagram or Google, are governed by those services’ own privacy practices. Unifolllow is independent of Instagram and Meta.

Data security and retention

No internet service can promise absolute security. Keep personal exports in a location you control and delete local copies when you no longer need them. Because the checker is designed not to receive selected export files, it has no server-side export archive for us to retain or remove.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as the site changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when we do. Questions can be sent through the contact page; please do not attach personal export data.

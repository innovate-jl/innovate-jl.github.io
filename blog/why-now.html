Why a file-based comparison is more transparent

Ask what a tool needs from you

When a service promises to analyze your social account, start with a basic question: what information must leave my device for that promise to work? Some tasks need an account connection. A simple comparison of two files does not.

Instagram lets account holders request their own information. Using the relevant exported files means you can inspect the input, choose the files, and keep the rest of the archive private. That is a more transparent exchange than providing a password to a service whose storage and access practices you may not understand.

What “local” means here

With Unifolllow, the browser reads the follower and following files you choose and calculates the difference on the same device. We do not receive an Instagram password, connect to an account, or retain the selected export. Local processing does not make a result live: it remains a comparison of snapshots created by Instagram.

Transparency also means being clear about limits. A result cannot reveal why an account changed, prove an offline relationship changed, or replace the platform’s own settings. Read the full methodology before using the checker, and keep only the export files you need.
